`Nautilus.Interpolation.spline_fit` guarded the knot gap in one of the five legs of its tridiagonal system, and in neither direction consistently: `spline_fit_h` computed the magnitude, tested against it, and then passed the raw signed value on; `spline_fit_rhs` tested the signed value and so substituted `1.0` for anything negative; and the diagonal, lower and upper legs carried no guard at all. A reordered knot pair therefore built a system in which one leg saw `1.0` where three saw the negative gap, and no leg ever saw the actual gap through a guard written for it. Which leg substitutes depended on the input class -- an independent reimplementation of the five legs, built in review rather than committed here, confirms that a permuted vector fires the `rhs` substitution while the `h` leg passes the negative gap through, while a repeated knot fires both of them, since a zero gap is under the threshold on either test -- so the asymmetry was not a single reachable case to patch. Both exports now require strictly increasing knots, validated once at entry, and every leg uses the gap as given; `spline_eval` returns NaN for a vector that fails and `spline_fit` returns an all-NaN vector of the right length. The per-leg guards are deleted rather than reconciled, so there is one place where the gap contract is decided. The check sits in both exports because `spline_eval`'s out-of-range arm returns `ys[0]` or `ys[m-1]` without ever consulting the fitted second derivatives, so a NaN arriving from the fit would not have reached it: with knots `[0, 2, 1, 3]` it returned `0.0` below the range and `9.0` above it. Measured on four points of y = x^2 at chelis 0.19.1, permuting two knots moved the answer at x = 1.5 from `2.2` to `4.3125`, a repeated knot gave `2.0298913`, and a gap of one f32 ulp downward -- `-5.9604645e-8`, which no caller would catch by eye -- was accepted and gave `0.6875` at x = 0.5 where the sampled function is `0.25`. The requirement is on the sign of the gap and not its size, so a gap of one f32 ulp upward is still accepted: an ill-conditioned spline is still a spline. Strictly increasing knots are otherwise unaffected above the old `1e-30` threshold: results are bit-identical at every gap measured, including `1e-20`, `1e-29` and `1e-30` themselves, and a 480-binding sweep in review found no difference. Below it, values that the fit used to fabricate from a substituted unit gap now follow the real gap, so `[0, 1e-31, 1, 2]` with `[0, 1, 2, 5]` moved from `1.35` to `1.607143e30` and the same knots at a gap of `1e-38` moved from `1.35` to NaN. A NaN is therefore no longer diagnostic of refusal; it means either a rejected knot vector or an accepted gap too small for f32 to carry the divided difference. Nor is a finite result a promise that the gap was large enough: fitting at tiny coordinate scales can return a plausible wrong number, and scaling `[0, 1, 2, 3]` with `[0, 1, 4, 9]` and the query by 1e-31 returns `2.5e-31` where scale-equivariance requires `2.2e-31`. That pathology is pre-existing and almost entirely unchanged here -- the same scaling gives `1.8999999e-24` against `2.2e-24`, NaN at 1e-26 and 1e-28, and an accurate answer at 1e-10, identically before and after; only the 1e-31 row moved, from `1e-31` to `2.5e-31`, being under the deleted threshold, and it moved closer to the reference. `la_tridiag_solve`'s substitution of 1 for a pivot below 1e-30 contributes but does not explain the 1e-24 row, whose pivot is about 4e-24, so the governing quantity is the knot magnitude rather than the gap and it is not monotone in it. The book's knot requirements enumerate all three outcomes and publish no threshold, there being none to publish. A NaN knot is also refused now, where it previously returned a plausible endpoint. Fifteen tests cover the contract, eleven refusing and four accepting; the fixture that separates a strict test from a non-strict one is a repeated knot queried off the clamp path, because an admitted duplicate still divides by a zero-width gap and yields NaN at an interior query, which makes the obvious interior fixture pass for the wrong reason.
