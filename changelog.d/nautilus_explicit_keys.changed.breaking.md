Nautilus's seven sampling exports take an explicit affine key instead of the removed `Random` effect, and large Bessel coefficients have explicit source widths so `Nautilus.Special` can check under the newer compiler. This prepares the Chelis 0.18.12 pin migration; the pin and published-asset validation remain pending. Gamma, chi-squared, and Student-t sampling still have the known constant-gamma defect (nautilus#84). See nautilus#83 and chelis#2413.
