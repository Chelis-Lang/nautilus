Add `Nautilus.Rolling`: f64 rolling and expanding `sum`, `mean`, `var`, `std`, `min` and `max` over `List[f64]`, plus `shift`, `shift_fill`, `shift_clamped`, `diff` and `pct_change`, each with a `tensor_` entry point taking `&tensor[n, f64]`. Warm-up and `min_periods` match pandas, checked against committed pandas goldens by `scripts/check_rolling_parity.py`. Two module-local departures from the rest of the package, both documented in `spec/scope.md` and the book: the module is concrete f64 rather than `[prec: Float]`, and a position with no value is `None` rather than a NaN sentinel, so a warm-up cannot be dropped by a caller that only wanted the numbers. Addresses nautilus#85.
