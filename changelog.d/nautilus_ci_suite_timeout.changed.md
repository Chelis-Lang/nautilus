Drop the `--suite-timeout 2400` override from the native-suite CI step and from the documented local gate; `chelis test tests/` now runs at the stock 600s default. The override's recorded magnitude — roughly 800-940s on GitHub's Linux runners — dates from Chelis 0.18.9 and was never re-measured, but the condition it described was live until days before this change: the suite still exceeded the 600s default under 0.18.10 by this file's own 0.7.45 entry, and five of five successful runs at the 0.18.11 pin measured 658-772s. The 0.18.12 transition is what ended it. Across seven successful `ci.yml` runs at the current `=0.18.12` pin on 2026-10-03, each carrying `tests/rolling.ch` and 579-582 cases, the step took 116-230s, so the worst observed run is 38% of the stock default and the override was 10.4x it. Nothing needed sharding to fit, and `tests/README.md` rule 6 already forbids matrix sharding for speed. The 2400s cap was the real cost: it let a roughly 10x per-suite slowdown pass silently, which is the regression sensitivity nautilus#78 asked to recover. One consequence is not an improvement and is recorded rather than fixed here: the retained per-test `--timeout 600` now equals the suite default, so it can never fire first and a single hung test reports as `suite incomplete` without naming the test. Addresses nautilus#78.
