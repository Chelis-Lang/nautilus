`scripts/validate_book_examples.py` now checks every book snippet that shows its output. A `text` block must follow a `chelis-fragment`, directly or after one short prose line such as "`chelis eval --file` prints:". The fragment is evaluated with `chelis eval --file`, formatted in a temporary copy under a generated module header, with the page's earlier blocks in scope when it continues them. Every line of the output block must appear in the evaluator's output, in order; the page may omit setup lines the evaluator also prints. An empty output block, or one that follows no fragment, fails. Complete `chelis` modules are still checked with `chelis check`, now under a unique temporary file and module name so a book module can never overwrite or delete a real `src/` file. The single floor of 13 is replaced by two floors at the book's real counts: 9 complete modules and 35 output examples.
